Privacy policy

Updated 4 October 2026. Earshot is operated by Luke Mulholland, British Columbia, Canada.

The short version

What the microphone hears never leaves your phone. Your sound clips and your catch locations never leave your phone either. If you choose to sign in with Apple to use friends and the league, we store a small amount of game data on our server so those features can work. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

On your phone only

  • Audio. Earshot listens only while you are in a listening session (up to 30 seconds, or until you stop). Apple's on-device sound classifier works out what it hears. The audio is analysed on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Clips. When you catch a sound, Earshot keeps a short clip (about 5 seconds) on your phone so you can replay it on the card. Free players keep the best clip for each sound; Earshot+ keeps every clip. Clips are stored in the app's private storage, are excluded from device backups, and are never uploaded.
  • Locations. Location is optional. If you allow it, Earshot reads your location once at the moment you catch a sound and saves it with the catch on your phone so it can appear on your Sound Map. It is never sent to our server. Without the permission everything else still works. The Sound Map is drawn with Apple Maps, which is subject to Apple's own privacy practices.
  • Your collection and settings. Catches, cards, XP, streaks, achievements and settings are stored on your phone. Without an account they stay there.
  • Notifications. Reminders (for example about your streak or daily bounties) are local notifications scheduled on your phone. We don't send push notifications from a server and don't hold a push token for you. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Sharing cards. If you share a card image, it goes where you choose using the iOS share sheet. Earshot doesn't upload it.

What we store when you sign in

Signing in is optional and only needed for friends, the league, the feed, divisions and claps. If you sign in with Apple, we store:

  • Your Sign in with Apple account ID, held by our sign-in service (Supabase Auth). Earshot doesn't ask Apple for your name or email. This sign-in service is shared with another app from the same developer, so the same account ID may appear there. We don't use it for anything else.
  • Your handle, display name and emoji avatar.
  • Your level, total XP, Dex count (how many different sounds you have), shiny count and streak.
  • Catch metadata for each catch: the sound label, rarity, stars, whether it was shiny, XP earned and the time. Never audio, never location.
  • Friendships and friend requests, people you have blocked, and reports you have made.
  • Your division membership and the claps you send and receive.

How it's used

Only to run Earshot: showing your profile and stats to the people you play with, the weekly league and friends' feed, divisions and claps, keeping the community safe (reviewing reports and blocks) and keeping the league fair (removing impossible results). The classifier runs on your phone, so stats you send are self-reported; we may remove results that look impossible.

Who can see what

Other signed-in players can find you by your exact handle and see your display name, avatar and level. Your friends can also see your stats, your weekly league XP and your notable catches (rare, epic, legendary and shiny) from the last 7 days. People in your division can see your handle, avatar and weekly progress. Claps are seen by the person who receives them. People you block can't find or see you.

Purchases

Earshot+ is bought through Apple, which handles your payment. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from Apple so it can tell the app whether Earshot+ is active, and it doesn't receive your name or email from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Who processes it

Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service on servers that may be outside Canada, including in the United States. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps and the App Store. RevenueCat manages subscription status. They process data only to provide those services. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

On your phone: Me > Reset removes your catches, clips and progress, and deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone.

On our server: open the League tab, tap your avatar to open the account menu and choose Delete Earshot account data. That immediately and permanently deletes your Earshot profile, catches, friendships, blocks, division membership and claps. Reports are kept so we can review safety issues, and they identify accounts only by an internal ID. Because the sign-in service is shared with another app, Earshot doesn't delete the shared sign-in record (your Apple account ID); you can ask us to erase it using the contact details below. Signing out only ends the session on this phone.

Children

Earshot isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. The microphone and location are never used to identify anyone. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, delete your Earshot data at any time, and ask us about your data. This policy is meant to meet Canadian privacy law, including BC's rules, and we'll respond to access, correction and deletion requests promptly.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
