Terms & community rules

Updated 4 October 2026. Earshot is operated by Luke Mulholland, British Columbia, Canada.

Community rules

  1. Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate towards anyone, in names, avatars or anywhere else in the app.
  2. Keep names clean. No sexual, violent, hateful or offensive handles or display names. Many offensive words are blocked automatically.
  3. Play fair. Don't tamper with the app, fake your stats or abuse the league. Results that look impossible can be removed, and cheaters can be removed from leagues and divisions.
  4. Be real with claps and requests. Claps are for celebrating a friend's catch. Don't spam friend requests, claps or reports.
  5. Report and block. You can report or block any player from their profile menu. Reports come straight to us and we aim to review them within 24 hours.
  6. Zero tolerance. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove accounts that do it, without warning if needed.

Terms of use

Who can use Earshot

You need to be at least 13 to use Earshot. By using the app you agree to these terms and to Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA), which also applies.

Your account

Playing solo needs no account. Friends, the league, divisions and claps use Sign in with Apple. You're responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete your Earshot account data at any time from the League tab account menu.

What you share

Your handle, display name, avatar, stats, league results and notable catches are visible to other players as described in the privacy policy. You keep ownership of what you create and give us permission to store and show it to run Earshot. Your audio and clips stay on your phone and aren't shared by us.

Reports and moderation

Any player can report or block another from their menu. We aim to review reports within 24 hours and may remove content, reset names or avatars, and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules.

Earshot+ and subscriptions

Earshot is free to play. Earshot+ is optional and unlocks extras such as additional daily bounties and a reroll, a larger streak freeze allowance, card frame themes, the full Sound Map, the Clip Vault and deep stats. Earshot+ is offered as:

  • Monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions, and
  • a lifetime option, which is a single one-time purchase and doesn't renew.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may vary by country. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically at the same price for the same period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel a subscription any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone (or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Earshot+ until the end of the period you paid for. If a free trial is offered, it's shown before you subscribe, and any unused part of it is forfeited when you buy a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple. You can restore purchases from the Earshot+ screen. Apple's standard EULA applies to your purchase.

Microphone and your surroundings

Earshot listens through your microphone only while a listening session is running. Be aware of your surroundings, follow local laws and respect other people's privacy: don't use Earshot to record or eavesdrop on private conversations. Keep your eyes up while you play and don't use it while driving.
