Catching

How does catching work?

Tap Listen and Earshot listens for up to 30 seconds. When it is confident it hears a particular sound, that sound becomes catchable and a ring shrinks onto a target circle. Tap Catch as the ring lines up to earn more stars: three stars for a near-perfect tap, two for a good one, one otherwise.

Each catch becomes a card with a rarity (Common to Legendary), XP and a clip of the sound. A clear three-star catch has a 1 in 8 chance of being shiny. You can catch more than one sound in a session. If a sound fades before you tap, it slips away and you can try again.

Why wasn't my sound recognised?
  • Earshot uses Apple's on-device classifier, which knows about 300 sounds. Not every sound is on the list.
  • A sound needs to be clear for a moment before it can be caught. Get closer, and try to avoid loud background noise or several sounds at once.
  • Speech and music can drown out quieter sounds.
  • Check Earshot has microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, and that another app isn't using the microphone (for example during a call).
  • A few sounds are deliberately left out of the game.

If it keeps happening, try the sound again after it ends and restarts. Sounds are scored on repeated moments, not a single instant.

What do rarity, stars and shiny mean?

Rarity says how hard a sound is to find: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary. Stars reflect your timing when you tap Catch. Shiny is a special version of a three-star catch of a clearly heard sound. Higher rarity, more stars, first-time catches and shiny all earn more XP.

How do streaks and daily bounties work?

A day counts toward your streak when you make at least one catch. You earn a streak freeze every seven days in a row, and a missed day uses a freeze automatically if you have one. Daily bounties are three sound goals that change each day (the same for everyone on the same date). Clear all three for a bonus.

Clips, location and privacy

Where are my clips and what happens to the audio?

Audio is analysed on your phone and never leaves it. When you catch something, Earshot keeps a roughly 5-second clip on your phone so you can replay it on the card. Free players keep the best clip for each sound; Earshot+ keeps every clip in the Clip Vault. Clips are excluded from device backups and are never uploaded.

Does Earshot use my location?

Only if you allow it, and only to pin a catch on your Sound Map. Location is read at the moment you catch a sound and stored on your phone only. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Earshot.

What does Earshot store on a server?

Nothing, unless you sign in with Apple to use friends and the league. Then we store your handle, display name, avatar, stats and catch metadata (sound, rarity, stars, shiny, XP, time), plus friendships, blocks, division membership and claps. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Friends and League

How do I add friends?

Open the League tab and sign in with Apple. Pick a handle, then add friends by handle or by scanning each other's QR code or invite link. The weekly league ranks you and your friends by XP earned since Monday at midnight Pacific time.

Someone is being abusive

Open their profile menu to report or block them. Reports come straight to us and we aim to review them within 24 hours. Blocking removes any friendship and stops requests both ways.

Earshot+ and purchases

What does Earshot+ include, and how do I cancel?

The core game is free. Earshot+ adds extra daily bounties and a reroll, a larger streak freeze allowance, card frame themes, the full Sound Map, the Clip Vault and deep stats. It comes as a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone; you keep Earshot+ until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Earshot+ screen (from Me, or by tapping any Earshot+ feature) and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still doesn't show, email us with your Apple receipt.

I was charged but don't have Earshot+

Try Restore Purchases first. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I delete my Earshot account data?

Open the League tab, tap your avatar to open the account menu and choose Delete Earshot account data. Your profile, catches, friendships, blocks, division membership and claps on our server are deleted immediately and permanently. Your catches and clips on your phone are untouched; to remove those use Me > Reset or delete the app. The shared sign-in record (your Apple account ID) can be erased on request: email us.

I'm not getting reminders

Allow notifications when Earshot asks, or turn them on in Settings > Notifications > Earshot. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work without an account.
