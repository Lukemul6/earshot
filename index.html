Earshot

Collect the sounds around you.

Earshot listens for the world and turns every sound it recognises into a collectible card. A kettle, a crow, a passing train, a sneeze. Gotta hear them all.

Free on iPhone

How it works

  1. Listen. Tap the big Listen button. Earshot uses Apple's sound classifier, right on your phone, to pick out what's making noise.
  2. Catch. When a sound locks on, a timing ring shrinks toward the target. Tap Catch at the perfect moment for three stars.
  3. Collect. Your catch becomes a card with its own art, rarity, XP and a short clip of the sound. Fill your Dex, keep your streak and chase shinies.

Earshot+

Private by design

Audio is analysed on your phone and never uploaded. Your clips and your locations stay on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking. Playing solo needs no account. Read the privacy policy.
